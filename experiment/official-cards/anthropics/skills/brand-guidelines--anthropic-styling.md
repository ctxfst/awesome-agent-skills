# Apply Anthropic's official colors and typography to an artifact

## When
Use when an artifact needs Anthropic's look and feel, brand colors, typography, visual formatting, or company design standards.

## Material
An artifact that may benefit from Anthropic's official brand identity and style resources.

## Done
The artifact uses Anthropic's documented color palette, Poppins headings, Lora body text, and the documented fallback fonts while preserving text hierarchy and readability.

## Requires
For best results, Poppins and Lora are pre-installed. The Skill documents Arial and Georgia fallbacks and says no font installation is required. Color application is documented through python-pptx's RGBColor class.

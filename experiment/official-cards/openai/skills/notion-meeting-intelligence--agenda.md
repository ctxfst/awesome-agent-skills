# Prepare an agenda or pre-read from Notion meeting context

## When
Use when preparing meeting materials from Notion context, drafting agendas or pre-reads, and tailoring them to attendees and decisions.

## Material
Meeting objective, desired outcomes or decisions, attendees, duration, date and time, prior materials, and relevant Notion notes, specs, OKRs, decisions, and action items.

## Done
A Notion agenda or pre-read based on the appropriate meeting template, with source links, owners, timeboxes, expected outputs, risks, preparation requests, and cited research where useful.

## Requires
Network access and a connected Notion MCP server authenticated through OAuth, plus Notion search, fetch, create-page, and update-page tools.

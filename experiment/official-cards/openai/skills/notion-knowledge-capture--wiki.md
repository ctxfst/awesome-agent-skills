# Turn conversations and notes into linked Notion knowledge pages

## When
Use when turning chats, conversations, decisions, or notes into reusable Notion wiki entries, how-tos, decision records, FAQs, learning notes, or documentation.

## Material
The source conversation or notes, capture purpose, target audience, freshness requirements, content type, destination database, and any existing Notion pages to update or link.

## Done
A structured Notion page with the required database properties, summary, context, source links, tags and owners, plus relations or backlinks from relevant hub pages and records.

## Requires
Network access and a connected Notion MCP server authenticated through OAuth, with access to the appropriate Notion databases and page tools.

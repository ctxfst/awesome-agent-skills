# Synthesize cited research from multiple Notion sources

## When
Use when gathering information across multiple Notion pages to produce a brief, research summary, comparison, or comprehensive report with citations.

## Material
The research goal and scope, relevant Notion source pages, and the desired output format and audience.

## Done
A structured Notion document with key findings, supporting evidence, inline citations, a references section, source links, and recommendations or next steps when relevant.

## Requires
Network access and a connected Notion MCP server authenticated through OAuth, with Notion search, fetch, create-page, and update-page tools.

# Manage Linear issues, projects, and team workflows

## When
Use when reading, creating, or updating Linear tickets, or for issue triage, sprint planning, documentation audits, workload balancing, release planning, and status updates.

## Material
The user's goal and scope, Linear workspace and team access, relevant project and issue identifiers, and fields such as priority, labels, cycle, assignee, and due dates as needed.

## Done
The requested Linear reads, creations, or updates are completed in logical batches and summarized with remaining gaps, blockers, and proposed follow-up actions.

## Requires
Network access, a connected Linear MCP server authenticated through OAuth, and access to the relevant workspace, teams, and projects.

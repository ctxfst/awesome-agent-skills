# Upload-Post API

## When
Upload content to social media platforms via Upload-Post API. Use when posting videos, photos, text, or documents to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook, X (Twitter), Threads, Pinterest, Reddit, Bluesky, Telegram, or Google Business. Supports scheduling, a queue that can fill itself with the best times to post, paid partnership labels, editing/reposting/deleting live posts, analytics, FFmpeg processing, upload history, comments and replies, Instagram and Bluesky DMs, account-wide Instagram AutoDM rules, webhooks with a delivery log, LinkedIn mentions, Telegram chat and bot management, Bluesky reads and drafts, Google Business posts, reviews, performance and location info, and YouTube channel management (playlists, captions, comment inbox and moderation, channel settings, search, live broadcasts and live chat).

## Material
Upload content to social media platforms via Upload-Post API.

## Done
Supports scheduling, a queue that can fill itself with the best times to post, paid partnership labels, editing/reposting/deleting live posts, analytics, FFmpeg processing, upload history, comments and replies, Instagram and Bluesky DMs, account-wide Instagram AutoDM rules, webhooks with a delivery log, LinkedIn mentions, Telegram chat and bot management, Bluesky reads and drafts, Google Business posts, reviews, performance and location info, and YouTube channel management (playlists, captions, comment inbox and moderation, channel settings, search, live broadcasts and live chat).

## Requires
1. Create account at [upload-post.com](https://upload-post.com)
2. Connect your social media accounts
3. Create a **Profile** (e.g., "mybrand") - this links your connected accounts
4. Generate an **API Key** from dashboard
5. Use the profile name as `user` parameter in API calls

Base URL: `https://api.upload-post.com/api`

The `user` parameter in all endpoints refers to your **profile name** (not username), which determines which connected social accounts receive the content.
